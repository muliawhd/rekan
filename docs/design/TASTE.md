# Re:kan — TASTE.md

## 1. Product personality

Re:kan should feel:

- bold,
- academic,
- useful,
- youthful,
- direct,
- systematic,
- collaborative,
- slightly playful,
- confident without feeling corporate.

It should not feel:

- romantic,
- luxurious,
- overly polished,
- glassy,
- futuristic for its own sake,
- gamified without purpose,
- generic SaaS,
- AI-generated.

## 2. Visual identity

The approved direction is **academic neo-brutalism**.

Characteristics:

- thick dark strokes,
- obvious structure,
- visible containers,
- strong typography,
- high contrast,
- squared or slightly rounded geometry,
- purposeful accent blocks,
- limited decorative effects,
- clear alignment,
- deliberate asymmetry only when it improves emphasis.

The app should feel designed, not decorated.

## 3. Swipe-card influence

Re:kan may borrow the interaction familiarity of Tinder-style stacked cards:

- one primary person or collaboration candidate at a time,
- visible card stack,
- left/right swipe affordance,
- quick action buttons,
- profile detail expansion,
- post-match collaboration confirmation.

However, the visual vocabulary must remain academic.

Never use:

- hearts as the primary meaning of compatibility,
- romantic copy,
- dating terminology,
- age as the main identity attribute,
- flirt-oriented imagery,
- “hot or not” patterns.

Preferred language:

- Ajukan Kolaborasi
- Lewati
- Simpan
- Lihat Detail
- Cocok untuk Belajar
- Tujuan Kolaborasi
- Rekan
- Tim
- Room
- Proyek
- Kompetisi

## 4. Hierarchy over decoration

Every screen should answer three questions in under three seconds:

1. Di mana saya?
2. Apa informasi paling penting?
3. Apa tindakan utama saya?

If a decorative element competes with one of those answers, remove it.

## 5. Typography taste

Typography should be bold enough to support neo-brutalism but readable enough for long academic content.

Use:

- heavy display headings,
- medium/semibold section headings,
- regular body text,
- compact metadata,
- clear labels.

Avoid:

- excessive uppercase body text,
- too many type sizes,
- ultra-light weights,
- stylized display fonts for body content.

## 6. Shape taste

Prefer:

- strong rectangles,
- slight corner rounding,
- visible borders,
- flat fills,
- explicit dividers.

Avoid:

- fully rounded pill cards everywhere,
- glassmorphism,
- neumorphism,
- soft floating cards with large blurred shadows,
- decorative blobs,
- random gradients.

Pills are reserved for:

- tags,
- filters,
- status,
- availability,
- skill chips,
- compact segmented controls.

## 7. Color taste

Use a limited accent system.

Base:
- ink / near-black
- paper / warm off-white
- neutral gray

Primary accent:
- high-energy yellow

Secondary accents:
- teal/cyan
- magenta

Optional semantic green may remain for:
- success,
- verified,
- available,
- positive system status.

Accent colors must have meaning. Do not distribute them randomly.

## 8. Imagery taste

Profile imagery should support identity, not dominate the whole product.

Use:
- real student photos when available,
- neutral placeholders,
- academic/project imagery,
- simple monochrome illustrations for onboarding or empty states.

Avoid:
- aspirational lifestyle stock photography,
- romantic lifestyle imagery,
- irrelevant 3D illustrations.

## 9. Motion taste

Motion should communicate state.

Good:
- card follows drag,
- small rotation during swipe,
- card snaps back if threshold is not reached,
- bottom action gives immediate feedback,
- subtle page transitions,
- clear loading state.

Bad:
- bouncing buttons,
- excessive spring motion,
- long hero animations,
- constant floating effects,
- decorative particle effects.

## 10. Anti-slop checklist

Before accepting a screen, verify:

- Does it look like Re:kan, not a generic template?
- Is one main action obvious?
- Are accents limited and purposeful?
- Is spacing systematic?
- Are borders consistent?
- Are chips actually needed?
- Is copy concise?
- Are icons meaningful?
- Does the screen work without gradient effects?
- Does swipe have a button alternative?
- Does academic information outrank decorative content?
